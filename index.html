FAQs

B.Tech Admission FAQs

Which B.Tech programmes does SGT University offer?

SGT University offers 12 B.Tech programmes: Computer Science & Engineering; Computer Science & Engineering with specialisations in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Data Science, Cyber Security, DevOps, Full Stack Web Development and Software Product Engineering; Artificial Intelligence; Artificial Intelligence & Data Science; Electronics & Communication Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; and Civil Engineering.

What is the eligibility for B.Tech at SGT University?

You need to have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics from a recognised board, with minimum marks as per university norms. Your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for 2027-28.

Which entrance exams are accepted for B.Tech admission?

You can apply with a JEE Main score or take the SGT entrance test. Your counsellor will confirm the accepted exams and selection criteria for 2027-28.

How long is the B.Tech programme?

All B.Tech programmes are four-year, full-time degrees on campus.

What is the difference between B.Tech CSE and a CSE specialisation?

B.Tech CSE covers the full breadth of computer science. A CSE specialisation, such as AI & ML or Cyber Security, keeps the core computer science foundation and adds focused courses in that field.

Should I choose B.Tech in AI or B.Tech CSE with AI & ML?

B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence is built around AI from the start, while B.Tech CSE with AI & ML combines a broad computer science degree with an AI specialisation. A counsellor can help you choose based on your interests and career goals.

What labs will B.Tech students use?

The campus has 100+ advanced labs, including the Apple Lab @ SGT, a robotics and automation lab, an EV lab, a fabrication lab, materials testing labs and modern computing labs.

Is SGT University NAAC accredited?

Yes. SGT University is accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council).

Are scholarships available for B.Tech students?

Yes. Scholarships are offered under the university's scholarship policy for each academic year. Your counsellor can tell you which ones you may qualify for.

Where is SGT University located?

The campus is on Gurgaon-Badli Road, Chandu, Budhera, Gurugram, Haryana 122505, in Delhi NCR.
